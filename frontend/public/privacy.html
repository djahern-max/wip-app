Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-22.

This platform produces job cost and work-in-progress reports for construction and landscape contractors. It is operated by a CPA practice for its clients. This policy says what data the platform holds, why, who can see it, where it is kept, and how to end its access.

What we read from QuickBooks Online

When a client connects its QuickBooks Online company, the platform reads that company's accounting data through Intuit's API: the chart of accounts, customers and projects, vendors, items, sales transactions (invoices, credit memos, sales receipts, payments, deposits), cost transactions (bills, vendor credits, purchases, journal entries, time activities), company information and preferences, and the trial balance and profit and loss reports used to tie the platform's figures to the books. Access is read-only and covers only the client's own company. The platform writes nothing to QuickBooks and creates or edits no transaction there.

What we read from files the client uploads

A client's authorized users may upload estimates, timesheets and payroll registers exported from their estimating, time-tracking and payroll systems. Payroll registers contain employees' names, hours and pay; the platform stores pay rates encrypted and shows them only to the practice's staff and the client's administrators.

Why

The data is used for one purpose: job cost and work-in-progress reporting for that client, including the month-end work-in-progress schedule and its adjusting journal entry. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Who can see it

The practice's staff, and the client's own authorized users according to the role the client gives each of them. Every client's data is isolated from every other client's, and no user of one client can see another client's data.

Where it is stored

On servers and managed database and storage services provided by DigitalOcean in the United States. QuickBooks tokens are encrypted at the application layer, so they are unreadable outside the platform even to someone with a copy of the database. Uploaded files are kept in private object storage and are never publicly accessible. All traffic uses TLS.

What we do not do

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for anything other than the reporting described above. The only parties that handle it are the practice, the client and the hosting provider named above.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the client is onboarded with the practice. On the client's written request the practice deletes the client's data from the platform. QuickBooks tokens are revoked and removed as soon as the connection is disconnected, from either side.

How to disconnect QuickBooks

A client administrator can disconnect from the platform's Connections page, or from within QuickBooks (Apps, then Manage apps, then Disconnect). Either action ends the platform's access to the company; the tokens are revoked and removed.

Security contact

Questions about this policy, requests to delete data, and reports of a security problem go to admin@jobcost.dev.
