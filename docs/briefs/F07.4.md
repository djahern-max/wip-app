### Goal
Today a change-order work area can only be confirmed as a change order; nothing can mark it approved, so every job shows all its change orders as unapproved for ever (67 Elm Street: 53,704.13) and the revised contract can never include one. Rye Beach has lost money on change-order work that was done and never approved. This feature lets the project manager record that the customer agreed, under the project manager's own login, lets that be withdrawn, counts an approved change order in the revised contract, and puts every unapproved change order in one list with its amount and age. The platform records approval; it does not obtain it (D-42).

## D-44 · 2026-10-06 · EAC includes the estimated cost of unapproved change orders

**Decision**: EAC in the WIP basis counts the estimated cost of every kept work area on a job's attached estimates, including change-order work areas that are not approved (D-42). Approving a change order moves its price into the revised contract; it never moves EAC. Until a change order is approved, the job therefore carries its estimated cost with none of its price.

**Reasoning**: The owner's decision of 2026-10-06, confirming what F07 already does. At Rye Beach change-order work is often performed before it is approved, so its cost is expected whether or not the price is ever agreed. Counting the cost and not the price shows the exposure on the job and understates margin and percent complete rather than overstating them. On 6115758 the effect was 30,369.40 of EAC against 0.00 of contract on 2026-10-06.

**Affected**: F07 and F07.4 (no change in behaviour; now decided). F14 (percent complete uses this EAC). BLUEPRINT §8.2 and §8.3 say so in words. D-01 and D-04 unchanged.

### In scope
**Who may approve**
- `client_pm` and `firm_admin` may approve a change order and withdraw an approval. `client_viewer` may not. Confirming a work area's kind stays as it is (managers only, F07.1): a project manager approves; they do not reclassify.
- OPERATIONS gains the steps for giving a project manager a login with the `client_pm` role on a tenant (Plan question 2).

**The evidence policy key**
- A new policy key, `change_order_evidence`, with two values a person chooses between: "None required" and "A reference is required". No default. Description: "What an approval of a change order must carry (D-42)." It joins `REQUIRED_POLICY_KEYS`; Home's Policy line asks for it and the tests that count required keys change with it (five keys). Approving is refused in one sentence while the key is undecided.

**Approval and withdrawal**
- Only a kept work area whose kind is confirmed as change order can be approved. Approving records: the user, when it was recorded, the date the customer agreed (required; not after today in the tenant's time zone), who at the customer agreed (optional), a reference to the evidence (optional, or required when the policy says so), a note (optional), and the work area's price on that day.
- Withdrawing records the user, when, and a reason (required). The work area is unapproved again.
- History is kept, never edited: approvals and withdrawals are rows in a new tenant table (`tenant_id`, forced RLS, no update or delete in any code path). One audit row per approval (`change_order_approved`) and per withdrawal (`change_order_approval_withdrawn`), naming the work area, with before and after.
- An approval belongs to a work area by the same identity F07 uses to carry a kind confirmation to the next version of an estimate: the order number and the name both match. It applies while the work area's current price equals the price approved. If a later version changes the price, or the row no longer matches, the work area is unapproved, and the job says so in one sentence naming the approved price, the date and the price now (`CO_APPROVAL_NOT_CARRIED`). Approving again is a new row.
- A confirmed change order that is approved cannot be changed to original until its approval is withdrawn; the refusal says so.
- A change order priced 0.00 may be approved; it moves no figure.

**Figures (pure functions in `contract.py`'s module, Decimal, computed on read)**
- **Approved change orders**: Σ price of the job's kept, confirmed change-order work areas with an approval that applies.
- **Revised contract** = confirmed originals (as today) + approved change orders.
- **Unapproved change orders** = confirmed change-order work areas without an approval that applies.
- **EAC in the WIP basis**: unchanged by approval or withdrawal (D-44).
- Everything is as of today. What a revised contract was at an earlier period end, and the rule that an approved period does not change (D-42's third paragraph), arrive with period approval in F14; this feature stores the agreed date and the recorded time so F14 can apply it.
- Remaining to bill and `BILLED_OVER_CONTRACT` (F08) read the new revised contract with no other change.

**The unapproved change orders list**
- `GET /api/change-orders/unapproved`: every kept, confirmed, unapproved change-order work area on jobs that are Sold, In progress or Substantially complete: job, estimate number, estimator, work area order and name, price, days since the work area first appeared (the received date of the first version that carries it), and whether an earlier approval no longer applies. A total. Roles as the job pages.
- A page reached from Jobs by a link that carries the count, "Unapproved change orders (n)"; each row links to its job. `.table-wrap`, first column held, money right-aligned (D-22).
- Home: `JOB_NEEDS` gains one need per job with unapproved change orders, "n change orders, 53,704.13, not approved", after the F08 entries; and `CO_APPROVAL_NOT_CARRIED`.

**Job page**
- Contract section: Original contract, Approved change orders, Revised contract, Unapproved change orders, EAC in the WIP basis. The sentence under it is restated for D-42 and D-44.
- Work-area table: a confirmed change order reads "Change order, not approved" or "Change order, approved 2026-09-14 by <display name>". Its Action shows "Approve" (opens the small form in the row) or "Withdraw approval" (asks for the reason). Buttons show a busy label while they work (F08.2's convention). Below the table, "Approval history" lists every approval and withdrawal on the job, newest first.
- The Jobs board's two existing columns (revised contract, unapproved change orders) show the new figures; no column is added.

**API**

   **How an approval finds its work area, and why that is not a name match in a write path.** The key an approval applies by is `(estimate_id, order_no)`: the identity F07 and D-26 give a work area, the same one the kind carry uses. One difference from the kind carry: a kind is copied onto the new version's row at import (a write); an approval is never copied, it is one row read against the versions received after it. **Rule C (the owner, 2026-10-06)**: the approval applies only while *every* version with work areas received after the one it was made on carries a row at that order number that is kept, has the same name and the same price, and the latest row is confirmed as a change order (or is a kept row of an estimate attached as a change order). Any break ends the approval for good: a version 3 that restores the name and price after a version 2 changed them does not revive it, because the chain is broken. The service writes nothing on import; the state is computed on read from the approval row and the versions after it.
