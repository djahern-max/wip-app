# F09.2 · Estimates page: list and one estimate

**Roadmap phase**: B (an addition after F09.1; nothing moved) · **Blueprint refs**: §12 (frontend) · **Decisions**: D-22, D-47 referenced; none made here

## Why
The second design feature after F09.1. The owner approved a mockup of the Estimates list and of one estimate (2026-10-08) and asked for it to be built.

## Scope
Layout, style and display words only. **No figure, no API response and no permission changes.** Frontend only; nothing in `backend/`. Every figure on the page is the same field, formatted by the same function, as before.

**The list** (`Estimates.jsx`, `estimates.js`)
1. A page head: the title, one sentence ("5 estimates; 2 need attention.", counted from the rows the page already holds) and "Upload estimates", still the one primary action.
2. Status and Estimator each carry their label above the control.
3. Seven columns instead of nine: the estimate's id with its name under it; the client with the jobsite under it; Estimator; Status; Price; Versions; Attention. Nothing shown before is dropped.
4. With no estimates, a plain card with the two existing sentences instead of a one-row table.

**One estimate**
5. The name is the heading; under it "Estimate 6115758 · Sold".
6. The facts (Client, Jobsite, Estimator, Estimate date, Job, Versions, Price, EAC in the WIP basis) sit in a block of label over value; Price and EAC in the WIP basis are in bold. EAC in the WIP basis is the same value the cost-category table's last row shows ("Not decided" and "Not computed" in words, as there).
7. Attention (and Dismissed) moves from below the tables to directly under the facts.
8. The three tables keep their columns, order and totals rows. An omitted work area's row is muted; its Kept column still says "Omitted".

**Shared styles** (`styles.css`): `.page-head`, `.lead`, `.filters`, `.field`, `.select`, `.cell-sub`, `.empty`, `.facts`, `.panel`, for the later screens to reuse. No new colour; the accent's users are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The list and one estimate render at 1280 px and 390 px with no sideways page scroll (rendered in Chromium at both widths with stand-in data: document width equals the viewport; no page error).
- [x] One primary action on the screen (`test_one_primary_action_per_screen`).
- [x] The accent's users and the no-decoration rules are unchanged (`test_frontend_styles.py`, run function by function, passes).
- [x] `estimates.test.js`: the count sentence and the line under the name. `npm test` 84 pass; `vite build` succeeds.
- [ ] The owner's pass on jobcost.dev, tenant `rye-beach`: the list against the old page (same estimates, prices, versions and attention sentences); 6115758 against the old detail (every figure the same); the filters; at 390 px.

## The owner's choices, made by building the mockup as approved (each is one small change to reverse)
- Client and jobsite share a column, and the id and name share a column.
- Attention sits above the tables.
- Price and EAC in the WIP basis are the two bold figures in the facts.

## Not verified
- The backend test suite was not run (no database in the build environment). Run `make test` before deploying.
- Rendered with stand-in data only, never against a real tenant: long names, many attention sentences on one row and the "Dismissed" list were not seen.

## Discovered (not fixed here)
- With a filter chosen and no match, the page says "No estimates loaded for this company yet." (as before this feature). It should say nothing matches the filter.
- At 390 px the facts block is one column and pushes the tables down a screen; two columns would fit.
