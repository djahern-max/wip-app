# F09.4 · Configuration: all six sections

**Roadmap phase**: B (an addition after F09.3; nothing moved) · **Blueprint refs**: §7 "Configuration", §12 (frontend) · **Decisions**: D-22, D-47 referenced; none made here

## Why
The fourth design feature. The owner, 2026-10-09, on Configuration, Accounts: the "Show" menu was still an unstyled browser control. Configuration had only the shared paint from F09.1 (its brief listed it as not redesigned). The owner asked for all six sections as one feature and approved a mockup of them (laptop, and Accounts at phone width) before any code changed.

## Scope
Layout, style and display words. **No figure, no API response, no permission and no behaviour changes.** Frontend only; nothing in `backend/`.

1. **Section tabs** (`Config.jsx`): the heading is an h1 and the six sections are a row of tabs that marks the current one the way the main navigation does (D-47, point 1; the same `.nav-link` rule, so the accent has no new user).
2. **Accounts**: one sentence with "Re-run suggestions" and the primary action at its right; the API's counts as three figures (Active accounts, Unmapped with "n suggested" under it, Confirmed); "Done. All n active accounts are confirmed." when none is unmapped; the Show filter with its label above it, beside "Show inactive"; the ledger type under the account instead of its own column; Suggested and Unmapped in bold, Confirmed plain with who under it; editing opens one form across the row (on a phone a narrow stack that stays in view); an inactive account is muted. "Confirm all suggestions" is drawn only when there is a suggestion (it was drawn disabled at zero).
3. **Divisions** and **Burden rates**: one sentence; the add form as one card with its fields on a line, stacked on a phone; the list below. A division without a code digit reads "none".
4. **Cost categories**: one sentence and the list, no wider than it needs.
5. **Cost codes**: the slot under its category's name; a cell with no account has its code muted; one new sentence under the grid.
6. **Policy**: the keys are a list (D-47, point 3), one decision per item with a tick when decided, its value, who decided and when and the reference on one line, its status in words and its one action; "n of m decided" beside the sentence; deciding a key opens its form in the same item. Every control, option, sentence and rule of the form is as before.
7. Rows being edited are tinted; inactive rows are muted and still say "Inactive"; a list with nothing to show is a plain card with the existing sentence instead of a one-row table.

**Shared styles** (`styles.css`): `.tabs`, `.config-narrow`, `.control` (a text field styled like `.select`), `.form-card`, `.field-grow`, `.check`, `.checks`, `.check-grid`, `.actions-row`, `.show-inactive`, `.done-line`, `.facts-row`, `.table tr.row-editing`, `.edit-form`, `.step-editing`, `.policy-value`, `.policy-form`. `.filters` now aligns its items to their bottom edge. No new colour; the accent's users are unchanged.

**New pure helper**: `frontend/src/config.js` (`allConfirmed`, `confirmedSentence`, `suggestedNote`, `decidedWords`), with `config.test.js`.

## The owner's choices (made by approving the mockup, 2026-10-09)
- Policy is a list, not a table.
- The one status colour marks a decided policy key ("Decided") and a fully confirmed account map ("Done."), always beside its word. Suggested and Unmapped carry no colour.
- The decision numbers are dropped from two sentences a client admin reads: "(D-23)" on Divisions and "by decision D-23" on Cost categories.
- One new sentence on Accounts: "Each ledger account is given a division and a cost category. Confirmed mappings are what every report will use."

## Where the build differs from the mockup
- The mockup drew four figures on Accounts (Active, Unmapped, Suggested, Confirmed) as if they added up. They do not: the API's "unmapped" is every active account without a confirmed mapping, so the suggested ones are inside it. The page shows three figures and names the suggested count under Unmapped. No count changed.
- The mockup's Policy edit carried a note under the button; the build keeps the button disabled until there is a value, as before, with no new sentence.

## Not verified
- Nothing was saved: forms were opened and looked at, not submitted, and against stand-in data, not the owner's.
- A read-only role (client admin) was rendered for Accounts and Policy only.
- The backend test suite was not run (no database in the build environment); `tests/test_hygiene.py` could not be collected there. This feature changes no backend file.

## Discovered (not fixed here)
- The Policy descriptions come from the server and read like notes for a developer: "Period boundaries are tenant-local months.", "1 = January … 12 = December.", "(BLUEPRINT §8.3)", and the decision numbers in four of them. Backend copy in `backend/app/domain/config/policy.py`; the page-side hint for the WIP basis (`WIP_BASIS_HINT` in `policyRow.js`) repeats "(BLUEPRINT §8.3)" and was left to change with them.
- The count called "Unmapped" includes accounts that carry a suggestion, and the Show filter's "Unmapped" lists them too, while each such row's own status reads "Suggested". Wording for the owner to decide (for example "Not confirmed"); the API field and the Home checklist sentence use the same word.
- The Policy date is in the browser's own format ("10/4/2026"), like the Imports dates (F09.3).
- Customers still uses the old `.subnav` links; it is a later design feature.
