## F07.3 · Home says what to do next (set-up checklist and job path)
**Roadmap phase**: B · **Blueprint refs**: §1 (onboarding is a first-class workflow), §3 (principles 3, 6), §5, §10 · **Decisions**: D-22, D-35, D-37 (none made here)
**Status**: drafted 2026-10-04 by the owner's design partner; built 2026-10-04 (owner's answers to Plan questions 2 and 3 applied); the owner's pass on `rye-beach` is open (ROADMAP ◐). A patch under F07; it changes no accounting behaviour, adds no table and stores nothing.

### Goal
A person who opens a company knows what to do next without being told. Home today reads "Job cost & WIP reporting arrives with the next features". After this patch Home shows two things, each computed on read from what the platform already holds: the **set-up checklist** for the company (done or not done, one sentence, one link to the action that completes it) and **the jobs**, each with where it stands and the one thing it needs next. On a fresh tenant Home is the onboarding guide; on a working tenant it is the worklist. Nothing else on any page changes.

### In scope
**Set-up checklist** (one line each; "done" is read from existing data, never stored)
1. QuickBooks: connected and a backfill has succeeded. Not done: "Connect to QuickBooks" → Connections. Needs reconnect or a fresh backfill: the Connections sentence, with the link.
2. Suggestion rules: at least one active rule. Not done: one sentence saying the rules are loaded by the firm (OPERATIONS "Editing suggestion rules"); no link (there is no screen).
3. Chart of accounts: active accounts exist. Not done: "Upload the chart of accounts" → Imports.
4. Account mapping: no active account is unmapped (the count the Accounts page already shows). Not done: "n of m accounts to confirm" → Configuration, Accounts.
5. Policy (owner, 2026-10-04: moved before Burden rates; the test is the Plan's answer to question 3): the keys needed now (`timezone`, `wip_basis`) decided. Not done: "n of 2 policy keys needed now are not decided" → Configuration, Policy. Done: with a quiet note of the keys that wait for their features.
6. Burden rates (owner, 2026-10-04: the Plan's answer to question 2, amended): active divisions with a cost-code digit each have a rate in force on the tenant's today. While the WIP basis is undecided the line is not done and reads that burden rates are set after the WIP basis is decided. When Labor Burden is not in a decided basis the line is done with that note. Not done otherwise: names the divisions → Configuration, Burden rates.

**Jobs** (below the checklist)
- "n sold estimates to review" with the link to the review, when n > 0; "Upload an estimate" → Imports when the company has no estimate at all.
- One line per job that is not closed or cancelled: name, status in words, and the first of these that applies, as one sentence with its link: work areas to confirm (count); a tracked QuickBooks row or an In progress job that needs its link (`JOB_NO_LEDGER_LINK`); nothing needed. A Sold job with no link is backlog and needs nothing (D-35): it says so and shows no action.
- Tracked QuickBooks rows with no job (D-37) are listed after the jobs with their sentence and the link to Customers.
- No money on Home in this patch.

**Rules for the page**: D-22 throughout: sentences, never codes; at most one primary action, which is the first not-done line's; reads only (GET), nothing fires a write on mount. A line links only to a page the person's role can open; the set-up checklist is shown to roles that can view tenant configuration and is otherwise omitted.

**Docs**: OPERATIONS.md gains "New client set-up", the checklist in the same order with the command or page for each step (this is the runbook the Discovered list asked for). BLUEPRINT §9 or §3: one line that Home is the set-up checklist and job path (say which section fits). **Built: §1**, under "Onboarding a messy client … is a first-class workflow", the paragraph that states the priority Home serves; §9 lists reports with money on them, which Home is not, and §3 is principles. CLAUDE.md Vocabulary: no new term. ROADMAP: an F07.3 line under F07 (nothing moved). CHANGELOG entry.

**Dependencies**: none new. No migration.

### Out of scope
Any change to the pages Home links to. A screen for suggestion rules. Billing, cost, percent complete or any money figure on Home (F08 onward add their own lines). Persisting review items (F09). The firm console across companies. Writing anything to QuickBooks.

**Answers to the five questions**

1. **One read endpoint, `GET /api/home`, composing the same domain functions the linked pages call; the frontend never adds up anything.** "Every count equals the linked page's count" is true by construction when both read one function: the review count is `len(review_queue(db))` (what `GET /api/jobs` returns as `to_review`), the unmapped count is `config.service.unmapped_count` (what `confirm-all` returns, and the same rule the Accounts page applies inline: active accounts without a confirmed mapping), work areas to confirm is `contract.to_confirm` through `load_job_views` (what the job row shows), the job's link need is `job_issues` (what the job's Attention shows), tracked rows with no job is `ledger_items` (what the Jobs page draws), the QuickBooks state is `copy_status` and the connection row (what `GET /api/qbo/status` returns), policy is `POLICY_KEYS` against `tenant_policy` rows (the Policy page's `decided` flags), burden is `pick_rate` over `active_burden_rates` (the Burden page's rows). The alternative, the page calling the seven existing endpoints, would send seven requests on every open, leave the primary-action rule in the browser, and hand a client role 403s on the configuration reads it cannot open; and a pairwise test would prove nothing the composition did not already guarantee. The pairwise test is still written (criterion 9) so a later change to either side fails loudly. The endpoint returns sentences (D-22), the machine code beside each (as every other list does), a `link` naming the page and, for Configuration, the section, and `primary: true` on exactly one line or none. One `TenantSession`, reads only.

2. **Burden rates (owner decides).** *What F06.1 requires*: a rate in force on the estimate's pricing day for each division that has labor on that estimate, and only when Labor Burden (slot 20) is in the tenant's WIP basis; otherwise nothing is warned (`burden_issues`, owner's answer 5 of F06.1). A tenant-wide row (no division) satisfies every division (`pick_rate`). Rye Beach has one active row per division from 2026-01-01 (D-34). *The three readings the brief names*: (a) every active division; (b) active divisions with a cost-code digit, which are the ones an estimate can be coded to on the grid; (c) divisions with an estimate or job, which is empty on a fresh tenant and would call the line done before any rate exists. *Proposal*: (b), evaluated on the tenant's today (`tenant_today`: tenant time zone when decided, else UTC), naming the divisions with no rate in force in code order. **Owner's answer (2026-10-04): accepted, with two changes.** The Policy line comes before the Burden line. While the WIP basis is undecided the Burden line is **not** done and reads that burden rates are set after the WIP basis is decided; when Labor Burden is not in a decided basis it reads done with the note "Labor Burden is not in the WIP basis". A tenant with no division carrying a cost-code digit reads not done with a sentence pointing at Divisions (Claude Code, so the line never reads done on nothing). With the EX rate 0.1959 from 2026-01-01 entered, EX leaves the sentence under any of the three.

3. **Policy (owner decides).** *The keys as they exist* (`POLICY_KEYS`, no defaults): `timezone` (period boundaries; read today by Sold on and the estimate dates), `fiscal_year_start_month` (read by nothing yet), `wip_basis` (read today by estimate totals and EAC; D-04, D-05), `small_job_threshold` (read by nothing yet), `deposit_identification` (D-02 now fixes the rule, the `_DEP` number and the tenant's deposit item; F08 reads it), `fuel_surcharge_treatment` (D-04; the cost side, F10 onward). Only `timezone` and `wip_basis` are read by any code today. *Proposal*: the line is not done while either of those two is undecided ("n of 2 policy keys needed now are not decided", naming them); once both are decided it reads done, with a quiet second sentence "4 more keys are decided when their features arrive" so the owner sees them without being asked to invent values. The brief's alternative, "all six decided", would hold a fresh tenant on `fuel_surcharge_treatment` before a single estimate is loaded. **Owner's answer (2026-10-04): accepted as proposed.** The required keys are an explicit list, `REQUIRED_POLICY_KEYS`, in the pure module, with the comment that each feature that starts reading a key adds it there.

4. **Where the order lives**: `app/domain/home/checklist.py`, pure, no DB, with the two ordered lists as data: `SETUP_STEPS` (the six lines, in the brief's order, each a small function from a `Facts` dataclass to a `Line`) and `JOB_NEEDS` (the ordered rules for one job: work areas to confirm; needs its QuickBooks project (`JOB_NO_LEDGER_LINK` from `job_issues`); nothing needed, with the D-35 backlog sentence for a Sold job with no link). `next_need(job_facts) -> Need` returns the first rule that applies. F08 onward append a rule with its sentence and link and a test; nothing is rewritten. Tested on facts alone (the pure half) and once through the API (the composed half).

5. **What a client role sees.** The route is `can_view_jobs` (every role), so every role has a Home. The checklist is built only when the role has `can_view_tenant_config` (`firm_admin`, `firm_staff`, `client_admin`); line 1 is also omitted without `can_view_connections` (the same three today). The jobs part is shown to every role, with links only to pages the role can open: `client_pm` and `client_viewer` see the review sentence without its link (the review needs `can_manage_jobs`), each job's status and need as words, a job's line linking to the job (every role reads jobs), and the tracked-rows-with-no-job lines without the Customers link (`can_track_customers`). The response carries `links` only for what the role may open; the page draws a sentence without a link when there is none. The matrix test gets `GET /api/home` for all five roles, and a test asserts `client_pm` gets no `setup` key and no `customers` or `review` link.

**Tests first** (the checklist and the job-need order are domain code): `test_home.py` builds the fresh tenant, loads the Rye Beach rules and chart (`config_helpers`), runs suggestions and confirm-all, enters burden rates and policy, loads the sixteen sold estimates (`EIGHTY`) and the two EST6115758 files (`estimate_helpers`), makes the job, confirms all as suggested, sets it In progress, links `6115758 …`, tracks 1701 Ocean Boulevard; at each step Home's sentences are asserted and its counts compared to the linked endpoint.
