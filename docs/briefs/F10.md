**Reader** (D-50): the **firm** reads the Cost page (the tie-out and the unassigned list). A **client** reads one new block on the job page: cost to date by cost category, in plain words.

### Goal
Every dollar of cost that QuickBooks posts to a job-cost account is held on the platform line by line, with the job it was coded to, its account and its vendor, so that a job can show cost to date next to its estimate, and so that the cost side ties to the ledger month by month to the cent: cost on jobs + cost on no job + labor in the ledger = QuickBooks cost of goods sold by account. The platform stores lines; it never decides a job for a line (a line with no job is unassigned until QuickBooks says otherwise), never writes to QuickBooks, and never costs labor from the GL (§2.1; F11 does that from hours and rates).

### In scope

**One new table** (approved here, no further yes needed; tenant-scoped: `tenant_id NOT NULL`, leading index, `enable_tenant_rls`, RLS enumeration test extended; migration `0017` or the next number, reversible, downgrade guard refuses `wip`)
- `ledger_line`: `source` (`qbo`), `txn_type ∈ {Bill, VendorCredit, Purchase, JournalEntry, Deposit}`, `txn_external_id`, `line_external_id` (the line's `Id`; `LineNum` where there is none), `line_no`, `txn_date` (`date`, the document's `TxnDate`), `doc_number`, `gl_account_id` (FK to `gl_account`, nullable), `account_external_id`, `vendor_external_id` (nullable: the document's `VendorRef`, or the JE line's `EntityRef` when it is a vendor), `customer_external_id` (nullable: the line's `CustomerRef.value`), `job_id` (nullable FK to `job`), `amount` (`NUMERIC(14,2)`, **signed**: a debit to the account is positive, a credit negative), `description`, `memo` (the document's `PrivateNote`), `deleted_at`, `raw_record_id`. Unique `(tenant_id, source, txn_type, txn_external_id, line_external_id)`.
- What is **not** stored: division, cost category, `in_job_cost`, and whether the line is in the WIP basis. All four are read from `account_map` and `tenant_policy` when a figure is computed (D-22: figures computed on read), so confirming a mapping later changes every figure at once with no re-sync.
- `job_cost_line` (§7): a view (or one pure function; Plan question 1) that unions `ledger_line` with `basis = gl_direct` now; F11 and F13.1 add the other two bases.

**The normalizer** (`app/domain/cost/sync.py`, the shape of `app/domain/billing/sync.py`; idempotent; raw before normalized; a payload it cannot read is `skipped` with a reason and counted on Connections like the billing ones)
- Reads the latest raw version of `Bill`, `VendorCredit`, `Purchase`, `JournalEntry` and `Deposit` (all already held raw from the history start, D-48; no new fetch) and writes one `ledger_line` per line that posts to an **expense-side account**: a `gl_account` whose `ledger_type` is Cost of Goods Sold or Expense, or any account with a confirmed `account_map` row. Lines to balance-sheet accounts (A/P, bank, the deposit's bank line, a JE's credit to accrued payroll) are not stored. A Deposit is read only for lines that post to such an account (a vendor refund banked straight to 5xxx); its bank line is never a `ledger_line`.
- **Sign**: Bill line amount positive; VendorCredit line negative; Purchase line positive, negative when `Credit = true`; JournalEntry line positive when `PostingType = Debit`, negative when `Credit`; Deposit line negative (a refund reduces the expense). Amounts are the payload's strings parsed to `Decimal` through the F03 codec; **no rounding anywhere in this feature**: QuickBooks lines are already cents and every figure is a sum.
- **Account**: `AccountBasedExpenseLineDetail.AccountRef.value`, `JournalEntryLineDetail.AccountRef.value`, `DepositLineDetail.AccountRef.value`. An `ItemBasedExpenseLineDetail` line takes its account from the item's `ExpenseAccountRef` on the latest raw `Item` record; an item with none leaves `gl_account_id` null and the line is counted under "No account" on the Cost page (never dropped). Account is matched to `gl_account` by `external_id` (F05.1 attached ids by number); an account with no chart row keeps `account_external_id` and reads "Not in the chart" on the Cost page.
- **Job**: `CustomerRef.value` on the line → `job_alias('qbo_customer', value)` → `job_id`. Nothing else: not the header's customer, not a name, not the pool's spelling (D-30: matching is by id). A line whose customer row has no job (tracked or not, D-37) stays unassigned with `customer_external_id` kept, so the Cost page can name the row. When a job is later linked to that row (F07), the next normalize run fills `job_id` (order independence, as billing does for `customer_id`); the run is enqueued after every job link and unlink, as F09's refresh is.
- **Labor-slot accounts**: a line on an account whose confirmed `account_map` is cost category slot 10 (Labor) or 20 (Labor Burden) is stored like any other and **is never job cost and never unassigned** (§2.1: labor reaches jobs from hours × rate in F11). It is shown on the Cost page as "Labor in the ledger" and is the ledger side of §8.5 check 3.
- **Deletes and voids** (D-20): a CDC delete stub or a `Void` sets `deleted_at` on every line of the document; the raw versions stay. A document that returns with fewer lines loses the lines it no longer has (the F05 rule).
- Enqueued as the billing normalizers are: after each backfill page of those five entities, after each CDC poll that touched one, and once per tenant at worker start-up. The nightly check gains the cost side: current `ledger_line` sums by month and account against a re-sum of the raw payloads; a difference sets `drift` as today.

**Figures** (`app/domain/cost/figures.py`, pure; every figure a `Decimal` returned as a string by the API)
- **Cost to date** on a job = Σ `ledger_line.amount` with `job_id` = the job, `deleted_at` null, account in job cost (`account_map.in_job_cost`, confirmed), account not labor-slot, on or before the as-of date (today, until F14 brings period ends). Two forms: **in the WIP basis** (categories in `tenant_policy.wip_basis`; D-04) and **memo** (categories outside it: Equipment (owned), Vehicles (owned), Fuel, Equipment Maintenance), each by cost category. A job with no in-basis line reads 0.00, not "Not computed"; "Not computed" is only for a tenant whose `wip_basis` is not decided (`require_policy`, no default).
- **Unassigned cost** for a month = Σ lines on in-job-cost, non-labor accounts with `job_id` null, dated in the month, by account and by customer row (or "No customer").
- **The tie-out** (§8.5 check 2, the F08 pattern): for each calendar month from the history start, per cost account: on jobs + unassigned + labor in the ledger + excluded-basis lines on jobs = the ledger total for that account from `ledger_line`, which by construction equals the raw re-sum. The owner's pass compares the per-account month totals to the QuickBooks Profit and Loss by account, which is the external check, as F05.1's billing tie-out did.
- `COST_UNASSIGNED` (warn, §10): one exception per **document** (subject: the customer row when the line names one, else the tenant's "no customer" subject; `item_key` = txn type and id) naming the amount and the account: "Bill 98892, 1,250.00 to 5240 Subcontractors, is coded to Hess Residence, which has no job." or "…is coded to no customer." Registry row, stated fields `amount`, `account_no`; dismissable with a note (D-46).
- `COST_DIVISION_MISMATCH` (warn): one per document and job where any line's account division (from `account_map`) ≠ the job's division: "Bill 98908 posts 4,000.00 to 5240 (Excavation) on a Landscaping job." A pool job (D-30) is exempt only when the account's division equals the pool's own division; otherwise it raises like any job. Stated fields `amount`, `account_no`.
- Both codes are added to the F09 registry so the run queues them; the generators are pure and read `ledger_line` and `account_map` only.

### Where the build differs from the brief, and what it adds
- **A fifth part, "Outside job cost"**, on the Cost page and in the tie-out: lines on accounts with no confirmed job-cost mapping (unmapped, not in the chart, no account, or `in_job_cost` false such as the shared fuel accounts 5610 to 5630). The brief named four parts; without the fifth the per-account identity would not hold for those accounts, and the tick would not mean "every line read". For every mapped job-cost account the brief's four parts equal the total (asserted in `test_cost_tieout.py`).
- **"Any account with a confirmed `account_map` row"** is read as a confirmed row that maps the account as job cost or to a cost category. The Rye Beach rules confirm every balance-sheet account too ("outside income and cost of goods sold", `in_job_cost` false, no category); read literally, the rule would have stored the A/P and accrued-payroll lines. The chart's ledger types are "Cost of Goods Sold" and "Expenses" (QuickBooks' `AccountType` is "Expense"); "Other Expense" is below the line and not read.
- **A Journal Entry or Deposit line's `Entity`** of type Customer is the line's customer reference (a journal line has no `CustomerRef`); of type Vendor, its vendor (Plan answer 3). A Deposit line that banks a payment (`LinkedTxn`, no `DetailType`) is no cost line.
- **Voided** is read from the payload alone: `PrivateNote` starting "Voided" and every stored line at 0.00, with no need of the earlier version (the billing rule reads the history because a voided invoice keeps no amount at all; a voided bill zeroes its lines, which is enough).
- **The subject "tenant"** for `COST_UNASSIGNED` on a line coded to no customer: `exception.subject_type` gains the value (migration 0017 widens the 0016 check; the downgrade restores it `NOT VALID` because an exception's history is append-only), the queue shows "No customer" and its link opens the Cost page for a role that may read it.
- **The cost run** (`cost.run`, `app/domain/cost/run.py`): the `qbo.normalize` task applies each changed cost document itself; the run is the catch-all, full after a finished backfill, after a poll that stored an Item (an item's expense account may have changed) and once per tenant at worker start-up, and job ids only after a link or an unlink (two set-based statements). Each run that changed a row queues the F09 refresh.
- **The job page table** reads "From hours and rates, later" for the Labor and Labor Burden rows (the ledger never gives labor to a job), and the owned equipment, vehicles, fuel and equipment maintenance categories as one aside row with their estimated total; the estimated column is the F06.1 figure per category over the attached estimates (`ignored` excluded), the computed burden for Labor Burden.
- **The export** has the tie-out tab and the unassigned tab only (no labor tab; the page has the labor table).
- **F09.7** moved to directly after F12 in ROADMAP with the brief's sentence. The brief's Discovered list is carried whole from the live stub.

## D-51 · 2026-10-29 · Job cost comes from the ledger line by line; a line's job is its customer reference; labor-slot accounts are tied, never costed
**Decision**: Cost on a job is the sum of QuickBooks lines (Bill, Vendor Credit, Purchase, Journal Entry, and a Deposit line that posts to a cost account) whose account is confirmed in `account_map` as job cost, held on the platform one row per line with the account, the vendor and the signed amount as QuickBooks posted it. A line's job is the job aliased to the line's `CustomerRef` and nothing else: not the document's customer, not a name, not a rule; a line with no job is **unassigned**, shown by document and customer row, and reaches a job only when QuickBooks is corrected or the customer row is linked to a job. Lines on accounts mapped to the Labor (10) or Labor Burden (20) slot are held and tied (§8.5 check 3) and are never job cost and never unassigned: labor reaches jobs from hours and rates (§2.1, F11). Division, cost category and WIP-basis membership are read from `account_map` and `tenant_policy` at the time a figure is computed, never stored on the line. Nothing is written to QuickBooks.
**Reasoning**: The ledger is the book of record (CLAUDE.md); the platform's job cost must be a re-sum of it so that the cost tie-out holds by construction and every difference to the Profit and Loss is a line the platform could not read, not a line it reclassified. S-01 question 4 showed Ramp-synced bills carry the job on every line, so the line's `CustomerRef` is the one id the firm already enforces (§13.3); a header customer would charge a whole bill to one job when its lines belong to several, and a name is forbidden in a write path. Payroll posts to 5n10 in lump sums by division with no job (§2.1), so costing those lines would double count labor against F11 or leave it all unassigned forever. Reading the mapping at compute time lets the firm confirm an account mapping after a year of lines without a re-sync, which is what a set-up done by the firm over time (D-50) needs. Made in express mode at the owner's request of 2026-10-29.
