## D-49 · 2026-10-16 · The status colour may also mark a tie-out that ties
**Decision**: D-47 point 2 is extended by one use. The one status colour (`--done`) may mark the tie-out line of a report when every month held ties to the cent to the QuickBooks month totals, as the API's `balanced` says over at least one month; it sits beside the sentence the API gives ("Ties to the cent to the QuickBooks month totals (n months).") and never carries meaning alone. A tie-out that is still being checked, could not be checked, has nothing to tie out yet, or does not tie carries no colour; a colour for "does not tie" is still not allowed and needs its own line in a later brief. Nothing else in D-47 changes.
**Reasoning**: The owner, 2026-10-16, on the Jobs board mockup: "using some green is fine." The tie-out is the board's one statement that its figures are whole, so it is the one line on a report that earns the same mark a finished set-up step gets on Home and a confirmed account map gets on Configuration. The sentence stays the API's; the colour follows the API's own `balanced` field rather than a reading of the words, so the page never decides what ties.
**Affected**: F09.5 (the Jobs board's tie-out line; `tieOutDone` in `frontend/src/jobs.js`). CLAUDE.md "Interface conventions". Later reports with a tie-out line (the WIP schedule, F12) may use the same mark. `tests/test_decisions.py`.
