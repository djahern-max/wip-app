### Goal
Every sold estimate becomes, through a person's decision, either its own job or a change order on an existing job (D-03), and every job carries a crosswalk of ids to the outside systems that know it: its LMN estimate(s) and its QuickBooks project or sub-customer (§5). The job is the unit everything after this feature reports on: F08 reads billed and collected by job through the QuickBooks link, F10 and F11 land cost and hours on it, F14 puts it on the WIP schedule. Matching is by id, never by name: names, addresses and jobsites may *suggest* a link or an attachment, and a person confirms every one; nothing is attached or linked by rule, and every link, unlink, attach and detach is in the audit log. A QuickBooks project with activity and no job, and a sold job with no QuickBooks link, are visible as review items. Nothing is written to QuickBooks; duplicate customers are merged there by the owner, and the platform only points them out.

### In scope
**Tables** (new; all tenant-scoped: `tenant_id NOT NULL`, leading index, `enable_tenant_rls`, RLS enumeration test extended; migration `0011`, reversible, downgrade guard refuses `wip`)
- `job`: `customer_id` (nullable FK to `customer`; set when the QuickBooks link is made, or by a person), `name` (text; defaults to the original estimate's name, editable), `division_id` (FK, required), `revenue_method ∈ {fixed_price, time_and_materials, recurring_service, none, pool}` (D-24, D-30, D-31, BLUEPRINT §5; a person sets any value; default `fixed_price` when created from an estimate; only `fixed_price` reaches the WIP schedule; `recurring_service` and `none` carry no other behaviour yet), `status ∈ {sold, in_progress, substantially_complete, closed, cancelled}` (default `sold`), `sold_on` (date; the original estimate's `estimate_date`, else the date the job was created, in the tenant's time zone when set, shown as "sold on (set when created)"), `notes`, `created_by`, `created_at`. No contract amount column: revised contract is computed (§5, D-01).
- `job_estimate`: `job_id`, `estimate_id`, `role ∈ {original, change_order, ignored}`, `attached_by`, `attached_at`, `note`. Unique `(tenant_id, estimate_id)`: an estimate belongs to at most one job. Exactly one `original` per job (partial unique index), except a job with `revenue_method = pool`, which has none.
- `job_alias`: `job_id`, `system ∈ {lmn_estimate, qbo_customer}`, `external_id`, `linked_by`, `linked_at`. Unique `(tenant_id, system, external_id)`: one outside id maps to one job. `lmn_estimate` aliases are written by the attach action (one per attached estimate, any role); `qbo_customer` aliases by the link action (the `customer.external_id` of the row linked). A job may carry several `qbo_customer` aliases; a QuickBooks row maps to at most one job. Unlinking deletes the alias row and writes an audit row that carries the old value; detaching an estimate deletes its `job_estimate` row and its `lmn_estimate` alias; nothing else is deleted.
- `estimate_work_area` gains `kind ∈ {original, change_order}` and `kind_confirmed_by`, `kind_confirmed_at`, all NULL until a person confirms (D-01: the platform suggests from the name and the baseline, computed on read; a person confirms). A new version carries a confirmation forward to the row with the same order number **and** name; a renamed or new row starts unconfirmed (the F06 normalizer gains this step). No approval here (the sign-off feature after F07).

**Domain rules (pure functions in `app/domain/jobs/`)**
- **Review queue**: every estimate with `status_norm = sold` that has no `job_estimate` row is "to review". Pending and lost estimates never appear. For each, the platform proposes **new job** (the default, D-03) and lists **attach candidates**, each with its reason: "same customer", jobs whose QuickBooks customer is the parent of a project named with this estimate's id; "by client name only", jobs whose QuickBooks customer's name or whose original estimate's client text equals this estimate's client text (the name rule below); and "by name only", jobs whose original estimate has the same `jobsite` or `name` text (case-folded, trimmed, exact) as this estimate. Pool jobs are never candidates. A candidate is a suggestion in the UI and nothing more; the person picks new job or one candidate with a role.
- **New job** from an estimate: `job` row with the estimate's name, `revenue_method = fixed_price`, `status = sold`, `sold_on` as above; division suggested from the estimate's cost lines (the division digit that carries the most cost; ties and no cost lines → no suggestion) and set by the person; `job_estimate(role = original)`; `job_alias('lmn_estimate', external_id)`. One audit row, `job_created`, naming the rows written.
- **Attach** to an existing job with role `change_order` or `ignored` (`ignored` is for a sold estimate that is superseded or was sold in error; it stays attached so it never returns to the queue, contributes nothing, and the reason is required in `note`), or `original` only when the job has none and is not a pool. Audit `job_estimate_attached` with the role, note and alias. **Detach** returns the estimate to the queue and writes `job_estimate_detached`; detaching the `original` is refused while other estimates are attached. Estimates are never attached to a pool job.
- **Work-area kind**: on the original estimate of a job, each kept work area's `kind` is suggested: `change_order` when its name begins with "C/O" or "CO:" (the F06 `change_order_suggested` flag) or when the baseline comparison says it appeared after the baseline (D-01); otherwise `original`. A person confirms each on the job detail; unconfirmed rows show "suggested". An estimate attached with role `change_order` has every kept work area as `change_order`, taken from the role when read; nothing is written to its rows.
- **Revised contract** (D-01, read on demand, never stored): Σ price of kept work areas with confirmed `kind = original` on the `original` estimate; an original estimate with no work areas loaded contributes its header price, and the job says "no work areas loaded" beside it (a change-order estimate with none counts its header price as unapproved). Change-order work areas (confirmed on the original, or on attached change-order estimates) are shown as **unapproved change orders**, priced, outside the contract, until the sign-off feature exists. EAC (D-04, D-05, D-34) is the sum over the job's attached estimates other than `ignored`, each at its own F06.1 figure; if any of them is not computed, the job's EAC is not computed. A T&M job shows no revised contract (D-24). A pool job shows neither.
- **QuickBooks link**: for a job, candidates are `customer` rows of the tenant that are active, are a project (`is_project`) or a sub-customer (parent set, not a project), and are not yet aliased to a job. Suggested by three exact rules, each labelled: "estimate id in name", the row whose `display_name` begins with the digits of any attached estimate's id, with or without the `EST` prefix (`6611769 …` and `EST6611769 …` both match; the id must be followed by a non-digit or the end); "customer name", the row's parent's `display_name` (or its own, for a row without a parent) equals the estimate's `client_name` once both are case-folded, stripped of punctuation and of the generic tokens (client, jobsite, llc, inc, and, &), and not empty; "address", a street number and the next word of three or more letters (`378 dunbarton`) appear in both the row's `display_name` and the estimate's `jobsite` or `name`. Ranked: id first, then "customer name and address", then either alone; a row matching no rule is not suggested. Beside the suggestions, a search box lists any active, unaliased customer row by name, and the person links by id. The person links one; nothing is linked by rule. A row already aliased to a job is not offered. The link sets `job.customer_id` to the row's parent (the paying customer), or to the row itself when it has no parent, when it is null; unlinking the last `qbo_customer` alias clears it. D-35: the project is created when the first money moves, so linking a `sold` job offers to set it `in_progress` in the same action (one audit row).
- **Review items** (pure generators, computed on read, F09 persists; BLUEPRINT §10 names where §10 has one): `EST_UNATTACHED` (a sold estimate not on any job, with its age), `JOB_NO_LEDGER_LINK` (a job at `in_progress` or `substantially_complete` with no `qbo_customer` alias; never a `sold` job, which is backlog until money moves, nor a `closed` or `cancelled` one, D-35), `LEDGER_PROJECT_NO_JOB` (a project or sub-customer with at least one `billing` or `payment` row and no alias), `JOB_SECOND_ESTIMATE_FOR_CUSTOMER` (new; D-03: a sold estimate to review for which a "same customer" or "by client name only" candidate exists; carries the candidate list), `JOB_DIVISION_UNSET` (new).
- **Customer duplicates** (read-only list for the owner, who merges in QuickBooks; §10 `CUSTOMER_FUZZY`): pairs of active top-level `customer` rows whose names, case-folded with punctuation stripped and the generic tokens and suffixes (client, jobsite, and, &, inc, llc, co, corp, company, jr, sr, ii, iii) dropped, are equal, or where the shorter is the longer less exactly one token and a shared token has three or more letters (a first name removed). Shown with both ids, the count of billing and payment rows on each, and a sentence: "Merge in QuickBooks; the platform follows." Nothing is written.
