## (a) How a project appears on `Customer`

30 customers; 1 project; 3 sub-customers.

| | plain customer | sub-customer | project |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IsProject` | `false` (key always present) | `false` | **`true`** |
| `Job` | absent or `false` | `true` | `true` |
| `ParentRef.value` | absent | parent's `Customer.Id` | parent's `Customer.Id` |
| `Level` | absent | number | number (1 under a top-level customer) |
| `FullyQualifiedName` | the name | `Parent:Child` | `Parent:Project` |

- **A project is a `Customer` row with `IsProject = true`.** `Job = true` alone does not mean
  project: every sub-customer has it. `is_project` must be read from `IsProject`, nothing else.
- `ParentRef` carries `value` only (no `name`). `BillWithParent` is present on the project.
- Inactive customers are returned only with `WHERE Active IN (true, false)`.
- No change to the brief's `customer` table is needed: `is_project`, `parent_external_id`,
  `parent_customer_id`, `active` cover it. A sub-customer is a row with a parent and
  `is_project = false`.

## (b) References back to the project

**Invoice header.** The project's invoice has `CustomerRef.value` = the **project's own
`Customer.Id`** (not the parent's), and also a header `ProjectRef.value`.

**`ProjectRef.value` is not a customer id.** It is a different, longer identifier (6 digits
against 2 here) that matches no `Customer.Id`; it belongs to the Projects API, which this
platform does not use. The same `ProjectRef.value` appears on the project's expense line, so it
is consistent across documents, but it cannot be resolved to anything through the Accounting
API. **The usable link is `CustomerRef` → the customer row with `IsProject = true`.** Invoices
on sub-customers carry no `ProjectRef` (0 of 7).

**Expense (`Purchase`) line.** The reference is at **line level**, as BLUEPRINT §6.2 expects:
- `Line[].AccountBasedExpenseLineDetail.CustomerRef.value` = the project's `Customer.Id`
- `Line[].AccountBasedExpenseLineDetail.BillableStatus` (`Billable`, `NotBillable`, `HasBeenBilled`)
- `Line[].ProjectRef.value` sits **on the line, beside the detail object**, not inside it
- `Line[].LinkedTxn[].TxnType = ReimburseCharge` once the expense has been billed
- The `Purchase` **header carries neither `CustomerRef` nor `ProjectRef`** (0 of 36).

`Bill` lines have the same `detail.CustomerRef` shape (1 line in the sample, on a plain
customer; no bill touches the project, so `ProjectRef` on a bill line is unobserved).
This is F10's concern; nothing in F05 normalizes it.

## (c) What Change Data Capture returns for a deleted transaction: OPEN

No deleted record exists in the look-back window. **To do (owner): delete one invoice in the

(Intuit documents `status = "Deleted"` with `Id` and `MetaData` only; unverified here).

What was established:
- **All 16 entities of §6.2 are accepted by CDC**, one at a time and together in one request:
  Account, Customer, Vendor, Item, Invoice, Payment, CreditMemo, SalesReceipt, Deposit, Bill,
  VendorCredit, Purchase, JournalEntry, TimeActivity, Preferences, CompanyInfo. One poll = one
  metered read.
- Envelope: `CDCResponse[].QueryResponse[]`, each element holding one entity's list plus
  `startPosition` and `maxResults`; an entity with no change is an element without a list.
  Top-level `time` (string). Changed records arrive as full payloads.
- **`changedSince` 45 days back is accepted without an error.** The sandbox does not refuse a
  cursor older than Intuit's documented 30 days, so the platform cannot rely on Intuit to say
  "too old": the 29-day refusal in our own code is the only guard (plan question 3, as decided).

## Findings beyond the four questions

### 1. Sales-form lines, discounts and what "subtotal" is (bears on owner answer 5)
Line `DetailType`s seen on Invoice, CreditMemo and SalesReceipt: `SalesItemLineDetail`,
`SubTotalLineDetail`, `DiscountLineDetail`. No `GroupLineDetail` or `DescriptionOnly` in the
sample (both exist in the API). Every document has exactly one `SubTotalLineDetail` line and a
`TxnTaxDetail.TotalTax`. No amount anywhere has more than two decimals.

- **Discount** (2 invoices, 1 sales receipt): one `DiscountLineDetail` line **after** the
  SubTotal line, `Amount` **positive**, detail keys `PercentBased`, `DiscountPercent`,
  `DiscountAccountRef`. On these documents:
  `SubTotal line = sum of sales-item lines` and **`TotalAmt = SubTotal − Discount + TotalTax`**.
  So with subtotal as decided (before discount), `subtotal + tax_total ≠ total` whenever there
  is a discount, and the brief's `billing` table has nowhere to hold the difference.
- **An invoice with no item lines** (1 invoice): its only line is the SubTotal line; header
  `LinkedTxn[].TxnType = StatementCharge` (it was created from a statement charge). Here
  `sum of sales-item lines = 0` but the SubTotal line carries the amount, and
  `SubTotal + TotalTax = TotalAmt`. **"QuickBooks' SubTotal line when present" is therefore the
  right reading of answer 5; summing item lines would store 0.00.** Such an invoice produces no
  `billing_line` rows, so lines do not always sum to the subtotal.
- On every other document (29 invoices, 1 credit memo, 3 sales receipts) both identities hold.

### 2. A payment that applies a credit memo (criterion 10's identity)
One payment does. Its lines: one with `LinkedTxn[].TxnType = Invoice`, one with
`LinkedTxn[].TxnType = CreditMemo`; **both `Amount`s are positive**, and
`sum(Line.Amount) + UnappliedAmt ≠ TotalAmt`. **The identity holds only when the credit-memo
line is taken as negative.** That payment is also the only one without `DepositToAccountRef`
(1 of 16), so `payment.deposit_account_external_id` must be nullable. Every payment line has
exactly one `LinkedTxn`; no payment in the sample has `UnappliedAmt > 0`. Lines also carry a
`LineEx` object (name/value pairs), not needed.

### 3. Paging
`WHERE Id > 'n' ORDERBY Id` is accepted and **`Id` is compared as a number** (the count for
`Id > '9'` equals the numeric count, not the text count). Keyset paging is therefore available
for the backfill and is sturdier than `STARTPOSITION`: a delete during the backfill cannot
shift a record past the page boundary. `SELECT COUNT(*)` answers `{"totalCount": n}`.

### 4. Accounts
90 accounts, **none with `AcctNum`** (Intuit's sample company has account numbers off), as
expected: the Connections page will show all 90 under "accounts without a number" and no
`gl_account` row is created or touched. Shape: `AccountType`, `AccountSubType`,
`Classification`, `SubAccount`, `ParentRef`, `FullyQualifiedName`, `Active`.

### 5. Sales receipts and deposits
- SalesReceipt carries `DepositToAccountRef`, `Balance`, `TxnTaxDetail.TotalTax`, and a header
  `LinkedTxn[].TxnType = Deposit` once deposited. Fits "one `billing` row plus one fully applied
  `payment`" (owner answer 4).
- Deposit lines: 11 in all; 7 linked to a `Payment`, 2 to a `SalesReceipt`
  (`LinkedTxn[].TxnType`, with `TxnLineId`), **2 with no `LinkedTxn`** (they carry
  `DepositLineDetail.AccountRef` instead). Those 2 are the "money received without an invoice"
  lines the Connections page counts; "not linked to a payment" in the brief should read "with no
  `LinkedTxn`", or the 2 sales-receipt lines would be counted as well.
- CreditMemo carries `RemainingCredit` and `Balance`.
